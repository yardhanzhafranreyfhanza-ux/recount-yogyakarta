My Holiday Trip to Yogyakarta

Orientation

A few years ago, when I was still in elementary school, my family and I went on a holiday to Yogyakarta. We spent more than a week there to enjoy our free time during the school holiday. We traveled from Bekasi to Yogyakarta by car. However, we did not go directly to Yogyakarta because we wanted to enjoy the journey and visit some places along the way.

Events

We started our journey from Bekasi and stopped at several places during the trip. We visited places such as Purwokerto and Malang. We spent some time playing, sightseeing, and enjoying the places we visited. After several days of traveling, we finally arrived in Yogyakarta. We stayed at one of our relatives' houses there.

During our holiday in Yogyakarta, we visited many interesting places. We went to Pacitan Beach and enjoyed our time there with my family. We also went swimming at a water park in Yogyakarta. Besides that, we visited the town square in Solo and the town square in Yogyakarta. We also took a walk and looked for souvenirs at Prawirotaman Street, which is one of the well-known areas in Yogyakarta.

Besides visiting tourist attractions, we also enjoyed many traditional foods. One of my favorite foods was sate buntel because I really liked its taste. I also tried rabbit satay for the first time at Telaga Sarangan in the Tawangmangu area. It was a new experience for me because I had never tried it before. At night, I also enjoyed some warm milk, which was quite famous in the area. After that, we returned to our relative's house because we needed to rest before continuing our journey home the next day.

Reorientation

The next day, we started our journey back to Bekasi. Although the trip was quite long, I really enjoyed the holiday with my family. We visited many places, tried different kinds of food, and made many unforgettable memories. It was one of the most enjoyable holidays I had when I was still in elementary school.
